# Lectures

There are no formal lectures; the material is designed for self-study. However, we’ll be available for live demos and Q&A twice a week, from {{ $course.firstSession }} to {{ $course.lastSession }}:

* **Mondays, {{ $course.hours }}:** Live demo of the previous week’s material, followed by time for questions and homework help. In past iterations of the course, these sessions have been very appreciated.
* **Thursdays, {{ $course.hours }}:** Open Q&A and homework help.

All sessions are in **{{ $course.room }}**, with {{ $course.teachers }}.

**Bring your laptop.** Parts of the sessions will be interactive, and following along will make the homework easier.
